# Vacation and Leave Policy

Applies to all full-time employees of Nimbus Data Sp. z o.o.

## Annual vacation

Every full-time employee receives 26 working days of paid vacation per calendar year.
Employees in their first calendar year receive vacation proportionally to the months worked.
Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Requesting vacation

Vacation must be requested in the HR portal (hr.nimbusdata.example) at least 14 days in advance
for periods longer than 3 days. Shorter vacations require 2 working days of notice.
The request is approved by your direct manager. If there is no response within 5 working days,
escalate to your HR business partner.

## Sick leave

If you are sick, notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.
A medical certificate (e-ZLA) is required from the first day of sick leave.
Sick leave does not reduce your vacation balance.

## Parental leave

Parental leave follows the Polish Labour Code. In addition, Nimbus Data offers 2 extra weeks
of fully paid leave to every new parent, which must be used within 12 months of the child's birth.

## Public holidays

Polish public holidays are days off. If a public holiday falls on a Saturday, employees receive
an extra day off that must be used within the same settlement period.
