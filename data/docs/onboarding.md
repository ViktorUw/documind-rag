# Onboarding Guide for New Engineers

## Your first day

On your first day you will receive your laptop from the IT desk on the 3rd floor of the Warsaw office
(remote employees receive it by courier before their start date). Your onboarding buddy will help you
set up access to email, Slack, GitHub and Jira.

## First week

During the first week you complete the mandatory trainings: IT security, GDPR basics and the code of conduct.
All trainings are available in the LearnHub platform and take around 4 hours in total.
You also meet your team lead for a 1:1 to agree on goals for your probation period.

## Probation period

The probation period lasts 3 months. At the end of it you have a review meeting with your team lead
and HR. Goals for the probation period are written down in the HR portal during the first week.

## Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00–15:00 CET; outside core hours you can organise your time flexibly.
Engineers can work fully remotely from Poland. Working from another EU country is allowed for up to
30 days per year and must be registered in the HR portal in advance.

## Learning budget

Every engineer has a yearly learning budget of 4,000 PLN for courses, conferences, books and certifications
(for example Azure, AWS or Databricks certifications). Expenses are reimbursed after submitting the invoice in the HR portal.
